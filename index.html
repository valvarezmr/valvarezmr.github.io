Valentin Alvarez

PhD student in Economics, University of Siena

I am a PhD student in the Department of Economics at the University of Siena. My research is in open-economy macroeconomics, with a focus on exchange rate dynamics and international capital flows under high capital mobility since the 1970s, often applied to Latin America and the Caribbean.

Before Siena, I studied in Argentina, including the Master's in Economic Development at UNSAM.

Research

Interests: open-economy macroeconomics, exchange rates, capital flows, Latin America and the Caribbean, economic theory.
