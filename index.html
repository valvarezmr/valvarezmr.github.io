Valentín Alvarez

PhD candidate in Economics, University of Siena

Google Scholar · ORCID · GitHub · LinkedIn · X

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Economics at the University of Siena. My research is in open-economy macroeconomics, with a focus on exchange rate dynamics and international capital flows under high capital mobility since the 1970s, often applied to Latin America and the Caribbean.

Before Siena, I worked for four years as a consultant at the United Nations Economic Commission for Latin America and the Caribbean (ECLAC), on macroeconomics, economic development and data science. I hold a Master's in Economic Development from the National University of San Martín (UNSAM) and a Licentiate in Economics from the University of Buenos Aires (UBA), where I have also taught since 2018.

Research

Interests: open-economy macroeconomics, exchange rates, capital flows, the international monetary system, Latin America and the Caribbean, economic development.
